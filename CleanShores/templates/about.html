About CleanShores

Citizen-driven, community-powered, science-backed — transforming India's coastlines and urban spaces one drive at a time.

Why CleanShores?

India generates over 62 million tonnes of waste annually, with a significant portion ending up on beaches, rivers, and urban drains. The existing challenge is not a lack of willing citizens — it's a lack of coordination, verification, and transparent impact measurement.

CleanShores bridges this gap by creating a trusted, verified platform where:

  • NGOs, colleges, and community groups can list cleanup drives with full accountability
  • Citizens can discover, register, and participate in verified drives near them
  • Every drive produces a public waste segregation impact report
  • Volunteers receive certified digital certificates of eco-contribution

Our Core Values

🌊

Ocean First

Every decision prioritizes the health of our marine ecosystems and coastal biodiversity.

♻️

Zero Waste Science

We promote scientific waste segregation — plastic, glass, metal, organic, hazardous — for maximum recycling impact.

🛡️

Verified Integrity

Only verified organizers can host drives, ensuring every event meets quality and safety standards.

👥

Community First

We believe local communities are the most powerful agents of environmental change.

📊

Transparent Impact

Every kilogram of waste collected is publicly logged and attributable, building a culture of environmental accountability.

🏅

Recognize & Reward

We celebrate volunteer contributions with digital certificates, badges, and impact hours — building eco-portfolios for students and professionals.
